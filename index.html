04 / From simulator to prototype

Turn the concept into
measured evidence.

The next milestone is a real camera pipeline on recorded clips, mat-labelled footwork data and landing tests that quantify dispersion before adaptive control is trusted.

01 / DRIVE

BLDC outrunner motors

Paired wheels generate ball speed and release variation. Log commanded and achieved wheel RPM.

02 / CONTROL

VESC-class controllers

Closed-loop motor control, current limits, fault reporting and a deliberate wheel spin-down path.

03 / LAUNCH

Wheels + feeder

Repeatable ball presentation, guarded wheel zone and a consistent release geometry.

04 / SENSE

Microcontroller + cameras

Timestamped trigger, synchronized views and a simple interface between perception and delivery planning.

RecordCapture representative clips with camera setup fixed.
LabelMark front foot, back foot, neutral and unknown on a mat.
MeasureRecord pace and landing coordinates for each delivery.
CalibrateEstimate dispersion and decision confidence from data.
AdaptEnable next-ball choices only inside validated limits.

Build principle: unknown camera reads should be ignored; match pressure must not override pace caps, launch-zone checks or actuator fault handling.
